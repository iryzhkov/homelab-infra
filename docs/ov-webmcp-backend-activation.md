## Recovery

Before user-write admission, preserve the failed candidate tree and restore only
the matched stopped-state baseline through the owned recovery path. Retain all
backups and operation evidence. Interrupted recovery, expired admission, partial
fact records or ambiguous service ownership require root reconciliation.

After any write-admission attempt, automatic old-image or old-data rollback is
disabled. Upstream account writes can discard the new lifetime identity. A plain
selection revert or image-only redeploy is therefore unsafe. Root must preserve
current data, assess forward repair or an explicitly qualified current-data
recovery, and persist that assessed selection before resuming deployers.

Full HTTP activation, per-host enrollment and genuine Claude/Codex acceptance on
laptop and PC are separate steps. The interactive stdio registration remains
until those checks and the retirement audit pass. Keep the backend maintenance
CLI and credentials needed by other consumers.
