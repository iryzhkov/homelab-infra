## Follow-up 2026-10-03 09:28 to 09:40 PDT: localhost:3100 hung on homelab

Reported by E26 after P1/P2: `docker compose restart gatus` on homelab hung twice for more than 10 minutes, `gatus` and `logging_grafana` stopped answering `docker inspect`, and dockerd logged Loki pushes to `localhost:3100` timing out.

Diagnosis (read-only, 09:28):

1. Loki's task ran on homelab, as pinned. `curl http://127.0.0.1:3100/ready` and `http://192.168.70.223:3100/ready`: `ready` 200. `curl -g http://[::1]:3100/ready`: `Connection timed out after 6001 milliseconds`. `getent ahosts localhost`: `::1` first, then `127.0.0.1`.
2. `ss -ltnp` on homelab: `LISTEN 4097 4096 *:3100 users:(("dockerd",pid=614,...))`. The routing mesh reserves the published port with a dual-stack listener in dockerd that never accepts; IPv4 is DNATed into the ingress before reaching it, IPv6 is not (the mesh is IPv4 only). Every connection to `[::1]:3100` sat in a full backlog until it timed out.
3. LogConfig of every container (`docker inspect --format '{{.HostConfig.LogConfig}}'`): homelab 47 containers on the loki driver, every one with `loki-url: http://localhost:3100/loki/api/v1/push` and no `mode` (blocking), including the Swarm tasks created during phase 5 (the running daemon still holds the old defaults); 6 on `local` (Loki's own task and the others with an explicit driver), 1 `json-file` (jocasta). normandy 18 and blackpearl 4 containers: loki driver, `http://192.168.70.223:3100/...`, no `mode` (blocking).
4. dockerd's log on homelab: 6294 `error sending batch ... status=-1` (connection failures) and 4 `status=500` since the F5 cutover, 821 in the 23:00 hour, 5262 in the 00:00 hour, 209 in the 01:00 hour.

So every homelab container's logs were stuck since the cutover: the Go dialer tried `::1` first and waited out the timeout, and a container stop waited on flushing them. The 23:50 to 00:00 Loki ring failure above hid this at first; it was not visible from `127.0.0.1` or the LAN address, which is what I had verified.

Fix: Loki's port is published in host mode (`stacks/logging/stack.yml`: `target: 3100, published: 3100, mode: host`), which puts docker-proxy listeners on `0.0.0.0:3100` and `[::]:3100` of the node running Loki. No daemon restart. Deployed `ansible-playbook playbooks/swarm.yml --tags stacks -e only=logging` 09:29:26 to 09:29:54 (stop-first; Loki's port down about 28 s, ready at 09:30:10). Afterwards on homelab: `localhost`, `127.0.0.1`, `[::1]` and `192.168.70.223` all answer in under 1 ms; from normandy `192.168.70.223:3100` answers; no Loki push error in dockerd's log after 09:29:40; container streams arriving again (gatus, forgejo, grafana, obsidian, ombi, openviking, miniflux and others within three minutes).

Every client names homelab's address (daemon.json, promtail, toolfeedback), so Loki must stay on homelab: do not move `logging` with swarm-move.yml, and a homelab drain stops log collection (as the plan already said). Grafana (3200) and InfluxDB (8087) stay on the routing mesh.

### Non-blocking logging everywhere, without a daemon restart (2026-10-03, 09:38 to 10:05 PDT)

The coordinator's requirement for phase 7: every container on normandy and black-pearl at least, in non-blocking mode. Done on all three hosts; no daemon was restarted.

Design:

- Swarm services: `roles/swarm/tasks/stack.yml` gives every service of the rendered stack that names no log driver `swarm_stack_logging` (`roles/swarm/defaults/main.yml`: loki, `http://<bootstrap manager>:3100/loki/api/v1/push`, `mode: non-blocking`, `max-buffer-size: 4m`, `loki-retries 3`, `loki-batch-size 400`, `max-size 50m`, `max-file 3`). Services with their own logging keep it (Loki and the four Authentik services use `local`). Proof on the first stack (excalidraw, 09:45:48): `docker service inspect` shows the LogDriver, and the new task on normandy has `mode: non-blocking` plus the daemon's own `loki-external-labels: host=omarchy-normandy`, so a task created after the spec change gets it with no daemon restart.
- Compose projects: `roles/compose_stack` writes `docker-compose.override.yml` beside the Compose file (Compose reads it by itself), giving every service without its own `logging:` the host's daemon.json log-opts verbatim, only where daemon.json already says loki and non-blocking, and records the file in the drift manifest's `config_files`. The adopted Compose files stay byte-identical. Off on the edge proxies (`compose_logging_override` default; phase 4 owns them, and they stop with proxmox-a anyway). jocasta keeps its explicit json-file driver and gets no override.
- Fix needed on the way: the cv-site redeploy refused ("cv-site still runs as a Compose project"). The image was built by `docker compose build` and carries `com.docker.compose.project=cv-site` itself, so its own Swarm task matched the Compose-project filter. `stack.yml` now counts only containers without `com.docker.swarm.service.id`.
