## Plan

- One stack, three services, all constrained to `pin.logging`, so they always share a node and reach each other by service name (`loki`, `influxdb`) on the stack network, as Grafana's data sources expect. Ports 3100, 3200, 8087 as before. Loki keeps the `local` log driver (it must not log to itself). Limits 1G each (working sets: Loki 165 MiB, Grafana 536 MiB, InfluxDB 326 MiB).
- Snapshot: sqlite `grafana/grafana.db` keep 48; files `grafana/` (without the database), `loki/`, `config/`, `influxdb/`. InfluxDB's mirror is crash-consistent at best; the daily `influx backup` archive remains the consistent copy once its script finds the Swarm task.
- Cutover: logs from every Docker daemon and promtail stop for the interruption (the Loki driver retries 3 times and then drops). Expected two to three minutes (1.8 GB, most of it copied warm before the stop). Neither the feed nor Jocasta depends on it.
- Verify: `https://logs.ryzhkov.dev` loads, Grafana's Loki and InfluxDB data sources test green (name resolution inside the stack, the incident lesson), new log lines arrive from homelab and normandy containers, Home Assistant and Proxmox metrics arrive in InfluxDB on 8087, `ansible-playbook playbooks/base.yml --check` first play (InfluxDB buckets) runs.
