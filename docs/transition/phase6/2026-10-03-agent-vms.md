--balloon 0 --cpulimit 3 --cpuunits 100`, 192.168.130.3, root 16G, `tank:100`.

- Both zvols are sparse: `refreservation none` (`tank/vm-111-disk-0` 400G,
  `tank/vm-211-disk-0` 100G). No GPU, no hostpci.
- cpuunits: the agents were built with 100, and lowered to 20 after review (below).
  homelab, normandy and public-api-b run at 1024, but black-pearl, Home Assistant and
  public-api set no value and so run at the cgroup v2 default weight of 100, which the
  agents' original 100 merely equalled. At 20 an agent gets a fifth of those guests' share
  and about a fiftieth of the 1024 guests' under contention. cpulimit caps agent-a at 8 of
  proxmox-a's 12 threads and agent-b at 3 of proxmox-b's 6.
- `qm resize` on proxmox-a warned that the thin volumes now add up to 382 GiB against a
  354 GB `local-lvm` pool; the pool was already overcommitted (56.6% used) before this.
