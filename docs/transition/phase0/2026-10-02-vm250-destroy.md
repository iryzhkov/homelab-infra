
Answer: VM 250 is gone, and its config and both logical volumes are removed. The `local-lvm` thin pool on proxmox-b went from 82786736 KiB used (55.77 %) to 81376527 KiB (54.82 %), freeing 1410209 KiB (about 1.3 GiB). The 12 GB disk was only 11.19 % allocated, so little space came back.

## Implications for later phases

- The ten VM 250 archives on `nas-backup` (about 10.9 GB) will not be pruned by the nightly job now that the guest no longer exists. Remove them by hand once they are no longer wanted.
