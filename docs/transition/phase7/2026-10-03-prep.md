## Fixes to swarm-move.yml (lead's input)

- `logging` cannot move: `swarm-move.yml -e stack=logging -e to=normandy --check` now fails at
  once with "logging does not move: Loki publishes 3100 in host mode on homelab ...". The drain
  skips it and reports it as staying.
- Leftovers of the autobrr rehearsal. The design kept the old node's `.moved/<stack>-<stamp>`
  until the stack next leaves that node, and nothing removed the rest: on homelab
  `/srv/swarm/.moved/autobrr-20261003-000449` (older than the live data) and
  `/mnt/docker-data/peer-snapshots/normandy/autobrr` (normandy's copy, never refreshed again);
  on normandy `/mnt/data/snapshots/autobrr` (no longer pruned by the hourly job, which acts only
  on owned stacks) and `/mnt/data/peer-snapshots/homelab/autobrr/final` (the first move's cold
  copy; the hourly job never sends `final/`). Answer: yes, the move should have removed them.
  The tidy, after the stack answers healthy on the new node, removes exactly those four kinds
  and keeps the old node's fresh `.moved/<stack>-<stamp>` as the rollback copy. It refuses
  unless the pin label and `.swarm-owner` both name the owner and the other node has no live
  copy (`-e to=normandy` for autobrr: refused, "pin.autobrr is on ['homelab']").
- Cleaned through the playbook, 10:16:10: `swarm-move.yml -e stack=autobrr -e to=homelab -e
  tidy_only=true` removed the four paths above (`ok=11 changed=2`). Remaining by design:
  normandy `/srv/swarm/.moved/autobrr-20261003-000540`. No other `final/` directory exists on
  either worker.
- Consequence of the tidy: a move discards the old owner's hourly snapshot history for that stack
  (local and peer copy); the live data, the cold copy in `.moved`, and B2's hidden versions
  (30 days) remain, and the new owner's first hourly snapshot follows within the hour.
